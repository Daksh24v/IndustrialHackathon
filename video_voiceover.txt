DEMO

This factory has two floors, eighty rooms and five hundred machines. Meet Finni: Google Maps for every building.

Where's the coffee machine? Finni asks one thing: what can you see near you? Tap the air compressor. Turn right into the main aisle, past the shoe cabinet, the kitchen is on your left. Press Start, and it walks you there.

The office lift is broken. Finni reports it for everyone. The lift turns red on every map, and maintenance is told.

Driving a forklift? Aisle B2 is blocked. Finni goes around it, and tells you why: twenty metres longer, under a minute.

In an emergency, press the shield. Nearest exit: one tap.


HOW IT WORKS

How it works. Claude is only the conversation layer. It understands the person, asks at most one question, and calls tools. It never guesses a distance.

Every answer comes from a deterministic core: a graph of rooms, doors, corridors, stairs and lifts, with real walking paths inside each room, and a shortest-path search.

A wheelchair, a trolley or a forklift only changes the graph: door widths, no steps, and a forklift only through halls and aisles.

Give Finni only floor plans, and it reads the drawings, lines up the floors, and finds the stairs, even when none are drawn.

On buildings we locked away and never tuned on: thirty-five of thirty-six questions right. Any building, one command. Finni.
