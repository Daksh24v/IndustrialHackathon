# Video script (2 minutes)

Record the screen with the website on the factory (dark look, laptop width), following [DEMO.md](DEMO.md): steps
1–2, the 60-second story steps 1–3, and step 9 (Forklift). The voiceover alone, for ElevenLabs, is in
`video_voiceover.txt` (about 250 words, 1:50–2:05 at a calm pace).

| Time | On screen (record with DEMO.md, dark look, laptop width) | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Opening flies into the factory; the floor stack turns | This is a factory. Two floors, eighty rooms, five hundred machines and things. And the only map is a PDF on a wall. Meet Finni: Google Maps for every building. |
| 0:10–0:18 | Drag to turn the building, hover a floor, click to fly in | Finni opens on the building itself. Every floor is a slab you can turn, tilt and step into. |

Finni never asks for room numbers. It asks one thing: what can you see near you? Tap the air compressor, and there's your route, in words people actually follow. Turn right into the main aisle, past the shoe cabinet, the kitchen is on your left. Press Start, and it walks you there, step by step. |
| 0:42–1:02 | Mic: "the office lift is broken" → reply → lift red, red line under the search bar, maintenance inbox; second window: wheelchair to Office 1.04 → "no step-free way right now" | Now something breaks. The office lift is broken. Finni reports it for everyone. | 1:50–2:00 | Back to the floor stack turning slowly, FINNI mark | Any building. Any person. Any vehicle. Finni. |

**For ElevenLabs:** paste `video_voiceover.txt` as it is ("Finni" is said "Finny"; the blank lines between
scenes give natural pauses). Speed 1.0, a calm, clear voice. Record the screen first, then cut it to the voice.
